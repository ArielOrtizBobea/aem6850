Data behind the session 13 figures. Downloaded 2026-10-08.

chetty1_increase_admin.csv
  Share of empirical micro papers in four journals using administrative
  data, 1980-2010, read off Raj Chetty's NBER Summer Institute 2012 slides
  ("Time Trends in the Use of Administrative Data for Empirical Research",
  https://www.nber.org/conferences/2012/SI2012/LS/ChettySlides.pdf) and
  digitised by Lars Vilhuber: Zenodo record 1453345,
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1453345 (open licence, see the record).

county_outcomes_simple.csv
  Opportunity Atlas, county-level outcomes by parent income, race and
  gender (Chetty, Friedman, Hendren, Jones & Porter). Public download from
  https://opportunityinsights.org/data/ (file
  wp-content/uploads/2018/10/county_outcomes_simple.csv). Built from tax
  and Census records; only aggregates are released.

affinity_daily.csv
  Opportunity Insights Economic Tracker, "Affinity - National - Daily.csv"
  from https://github.com/OpportunityInsights/EconomicTracker (data/),
  card spending relative to January 2020, with spend_all_q1..q4 the
  ZIP-code median-income quartiles (q1 lowest). Chetty, Friedman, Stepner
  & the Opportunity Insights Team (QJE, 2024), https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjad048.

  Licence of the digitised Chetty series: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (the Zenodo
  record's LICENSE file), so F2 carries the same licence and the credit.
