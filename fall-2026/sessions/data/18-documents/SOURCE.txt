18-documents: data for AEM 6850, session 18 (Documents & scans), Thu Oct 29, 2026.
Assembled 2026-10-27.

h41.pdf
  Federal Reserve statistical release H.4.1, "Factors Affecting Reserve
  Balances", 3 October 2024. Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System,
  https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h41/20241003/h41.pdf (192,780 bytes,
  11 pages, made in Microsoft Word). US federal work, public domain.
  Table 5 (consolidated statement) pages 6-7; Table 6 (each Reserve Bank)
  pages 8-9. Downloaded 2026-10-27.

comp1899_p.pdf, comp1899_p.png
  One page of the Annual Report of the Comptroller of the Currency, 1899,
  Volume 1: PDF page 693, printed page 646, Table XVII ("Aggregate resources,
  expressed in millions of dollars, of national banks on June 30, 1899, and
  State, savings, private banks, and loan and trust companies at date of
  latest returns to this bureau"). Cut with pdftools::pdf_subset() from
  https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/comp/1890s/1899/compcurr_1899_vol1.pdf
  (FRASER, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis; 907 pages; 300-dpi scans with
  an OCR text layer added by the archive). US federal work. The PNG is the
  page rendered at 300 dpi. Downloaded 2026-10-27.

sheets/
  Ten-year sheets of monthly rainfall at Abbotsbury Castle, Dorset, England,
  from the Met Office archive, as published by Rainfall Rescue:
  https://github.com/ed-hawkins/rainfall-rescue, DATA/ABBOTSBURY-CASTLE/
  ABBOTSBURY-CASTLE.pdf (six pages, JPEG at 170 ppi, extracted with
  pdfimages). Licence CC BY 4.0 (Zenodo record 5770390, v2.1.0). Pages 1-6
  are the 1860s, 1870s, 1880s, 1890s, 1910s and 1920s sheets. crops/ holds
  130 x 90 px crops (at 3x) of the cells discussed in class.
  Paper: Hawkins et al., Geoscience Data Journal, 2023,
  https://doi.org/10.1002/gdj3.157 (CC BY 4.0).

volunteers/
  The volunteers' transcriptions of each sheet (TYRain_*.csv and the
  ERROR_ file for the 1880s), and the combined station series
  (ABBOTSBURY-CASTLE.csv), from the same repository, unchanged. -999 marks
  a cell the volunteers could not read. The ERROR_ prefix marks a sheet
  where a year's months do not add up to the transcribed total.

cache/
  Raw replies of a vision model (one JSON file per sheet and run, and one
  for the 1899 page), made 2026-10-27 with the ellmer package from R, the
  model's default settings, prompts in code/prompt_sheet.txt and
  code/make_cache_1899.R; log.csv records seconds and tokens per call.

ocr/
  tesseract (eng) output for the pages used, and the page images it ran on.
  For anyone whose tesseract install fails.

agent/
  The CSV files and transcripts of two coding-agent runs made before class
  (2026-10-27): Table 6 of h41.pdf, and the 1880s sheet. request_1880s.txt
  is the request typed live in class.

rain_volunteers.csv, rain_model.csv, table6.csv
  Long-format versions built by code/build_csv.R and code/fed_tables.R.
