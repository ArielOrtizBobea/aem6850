**How I recorded the sheet:**
- **Two figures cover two months each.** In 1887, January and February are bracketed together with one figure, 4.57. In 1886, June and July are bracketed together as 2.82. I put each figure in the month its line sits closer to (February 1887 and June 1886) and left the other month blank. The totals only match if each figure is counted once, so the observer meant them as combined two-month amounts. If you'd rather mark these cells in a different way, it's a quick change.
- **Numbers are copied as written.** Leading decimals stay as they are (`.52`, `.95`). July 1885 is written as `·00` (no rain), so it's `.00` in the file and not left blank.
- **I added a Means column.** The sheet has one, and its only entry is 31.015 on the totals row. The other cells in that column are empty.
- **The note at the bottom isn't in the file.** It says: "1888 Jan to March No observations. Sent subsequently." The values for those months are still written on the sheet, so I copied them as normal.
