Job placements & For hire: Ph.D. in Applied Economics and Management

Graduate students of the SC Johnson College of Business in Applied Economics and Management are actively recruited by U.S. and foreign universities for academic positions. They also hold high-profile positions in state and federal government agencies, international agencies, such as the World Bank and the United Nations, and at leading companies in the private and not-for-profit sectors.
