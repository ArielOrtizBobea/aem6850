# Paper figures for session 26

Each image is cropped from an open copy of the paper. Pages were rendered at 300 dpi with `pdftoppm`, or at 600 dpi for Jack et al. All files were fetched on 2026-11-24. The PDFs are kept in the session scratch folder and are not in the repo.

## p_hsw_fig2.png

- Citation: J. Vernon Henderson, Adam Storeygard and David N. Weil. "Measuring Economic Growth from Outer Space." *American Economic Review* 102(2): 994–1028, 2012. https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.102.2.994
- Printed as: Figure 2. Long-Term Growth: Korean Peninsula
- Page: PDF p. 9 (journal p. 1002)
- Open copy: published AER article posted by Brown University, https://s4.brown.edu/sites/default/files/2024-01/Henderson%20Storeygard%20Weil%20AER%20April%202012.pdf
- Fetched: 2026-11-24
- Shows: Night lights over the Korean peninsula in 1992 (left) and 2008 (right). The colour is the digital number, from dark blue (0) to yellow (63), with a 100 km scale bar. The caption and "Source: See Figure 1" line are left out. The "Universal Transverse Mercator projection" note is kept.

## p_hsw_fig7.png

- Citation: as above.
- Printed as: Figure 7. Growth in Fitted Lights versus WDI for Bad Data Countries 1992–2006
- Page: PDF p. 27 (journal p. 1020)
- Open copy: as above.

## p_burchfield_fig2a.png

- Citation: Marcy Burchfield, Henry G. Overman, Diego Puga and Matthew A. Turner. "Causes of Sprawl: A Portrait from Space." *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 121(2): 587–633, 2006. https://doi.org/10.1162/qjec.2006.121.2.587
- Printed as: Figure IIa. Urban Land in Atlanta, GA (Top Panel) and Boston, MA (Bottom Panel)
- Page: PDF p. 10 (journal p. 596)
- Open copy: published QJE version on Diego Puga's site, https://diegopuga.org/papers/sprawl.pdf
- Fetched: 2026-11-24
- Shows: Land-cover maps of Atlanta (top) and Boston (bottom). Yellow is urban land circa 1976 and red is urban land built 1976–92. Blue is water, dark green is forest, light green is range and grassland, tan is agricultural land, beige is bare rock and sand, and pale aqua is wetlands. Scale bar 0–20 km. The panels carry no city names, so the slide must say which is which.

## p_jack_figA3.png

- Citation: B. Kelsey Jack, Seema Jayachandran, Namrata Kala and Rohini Pande. "Money (Not) to Burn: Payments for Ecosystem Services to Reduce Crop Residue Burning." *American Economic Review: Insights* 7(1): 39–55, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1257/aeri.20230431. Online appendix.
- Printed as: Figure A3. : Visual Signs of Burning in Imagery: Example
- Page: PDF p. 3 of the online appendix (printed p. 23)
- Open copy: AEA online appendix, https://www.aeaweb.org/content/file?id=22187
- Fetched: 2026-11-24
- Shows: Three Sentinel-2 images of one study plot, outlined in black. They are labelled "Around harvest time" (10/28/19–11/3/19), "Just after burning" (11/18/19–11/24/19) and "One week after burning" (11/25/19–12/01/19). The plot goes from green to near black after burning, then to grey-brown. Each panel has a 0–0.25 km scale bar whose labels are very small. The note under the figure ("Imagery from Sentinel-2 showing a study plot at different points in the agricultural season") is left out.

## p_agm_fig1.png

- Citation: Jennifer Alix-García and Daniel L. Millimet. "Remotely Incorrect? Accounting for Nonclassical Measurement Error in Satellite Data on Deforestation." *Journal of the Association of Environmental and Resource Economists* 10(5): 1335–1367, 2023. https://doi.org/10.1086/723723. Figure taken from the NBER conference version of 5 April 2021, not the JAERE text.
- Printed as: Figure 1: Scatterplot of forest cover in 5 x 5 km cells across Mexico
- Page: PDF p. 12 (printed p. 11)
- Open copy: https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f147726/f147726.pdf
- Fetched: 2026-11-24
- Shows: Two scatterplots with one dot per 5 x 5 km cell. The x-axis is the proportion forest in 2010 from the Hansen data and the y-axis is the proportion forest in 2011 from the GOM data, both 0 to 1. The right panel marks in red the cells whose number of cloud-free images is above the median. The points fill the whole square, with heavy stacks at Hansen = 0 and at GOM = 1.
